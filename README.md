An exercise in rebuilding the classic arcade game Pong to experiment with AI opponents. The central challenge is tuning the computer player so it loses often enough to be beatable, without becoming obviously weak or trivial to play against.


If time allows additional variants of the Pong game may be added. 
Variants like:
Practice Mode A: Player controls both paddles simultaneously. Meant for beginners or people learning the controls.
Practice Mode B: The computer will always rally. And hit the ball near center. Good for practicing aiming.
Practice Mode C: Aim Assist. Current Ball Trajectory will show you how the ball will bounce off of the paddle.
Juggle Mode: Multiple balls on the field at a time. 
Blackout: Half the screen is covered. One cannot see the enemy paddle or ball. Significantly reduces reaction time.
Dark Mode: After the first hit, the ball is hidden (Enemy Paddle is still visible). Relies on guessing angles and trajectories.
